Delete Your Account — PSG JOB

PSG JOB (by Lailaolab) · Last updated: 4 November 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your PSG JOB account and the personal data associated with it.

How to request deletion

Send an email to lailaolabdev@gmail.com
Subject: Delete my account
Include the phone number you registered with so we can verify and locate your account.

We will verify your identity using the registered phone number and process the request. Your account and personal data are permanently deleted within 30 days of the request.

What data is deleted

  • Your profile: name, phone number, gender, date of birth, location
  • Education history, work experience, and skills
  • Your CV / résumé file and profile photo
  • Job applications, saved jobs, and interview records
  • For employers: company information, contact details, and logo

What data may be kept

We delete all personal data. A minimal record may be retained only where required by law (for example, to meet a legal or accounting obligation), and is deleted once that obligation ends.
